香港 · 日落後的夜空Hong Kong · just after sunset

那不是星星。That's not a star.

那是中國的太空站「天宮」。它在離地約 390 公里的高空繞着地球飛行，而你在香港抬頭，就能用肉眼看見它。It's Tiangong, China's space station. It circles Earth about 390 km above us, and you can see it from Hong Kong with your own eyes.

第一章 · 這是什麼Chapter 1 · What it is

一個在太空的家A home in space

「天宮」意思是天上的宮殿。它是中國自己設計、建造和營運的太空站，由三個艙段組成，2022 年底全面建成。太空人輪班住在裏面，進行地面上做不到的科學實驗。Tiangong means "Heavenly Palace". China designed, built and runs it, and it was completed at the end of 2022. Astronauts live on board in rotating crews and run experiments that are impossible on the ground.

第二章 · 太空生活Chapter 2 · Life on board

一天看 16 次日出16 sunrises a day

天宮每秒飛行約 7.7 公里，大約 90 分鐘就繞地球一圈。每一圈，它都會飛過地球白天的一面，再飛進黑夜，所以太空人一天會看到大約 16 次日出和日落。Tiangong travels about 7.7 km every second and circles Earth in about 90 minutes. Each lap takes it through daylight and then into night, so the crew sees about 16 sunrises and sunsets a day.

每批三名太空人通常在站上住大約半年，睡覺、吃飯、做運動，同時進行科學實驗。Each crew of three usually stays about six months, sleeping, eating and exercising on board while running experiments.

    觀測指南Spotting guide

    在香港用肉眼看天宮See Tiangong from Hong Kong with your own eyes

    不需要望遠鏡。只要時間和地點對了，抬頭就能看到。No telescope needed. Get the time and place right, then look up.

    1. 選對時間Pick the right time日落後或日出前一至兩小時最好。那時你身處黑夜，但高空的天宮仍被陽光照亮。One to two hours after sunset or before sunrise is best. You are in darkness while the station high above is still sunlit.
    2. 找一個看得到大片天空的地方Find a wide view of the sky天台、海濱長廊或郊野公園都不錯，盡量避開強光和高樓阻擋。Rooftops, harbourfront promenades or country parks work well. Avoid bright lights and tall buildings in the way.
    3. 讓眼睛適應黑暗Let your eyes adjust提早十分鐘到，少看手機屏幕，眼睛會看得更清楚。Arrive ten minutes early and keep away from your phone screen so your eyes can adapt.
    4. 找一點穩定移動的光Look for a steady moving light它不會閃爍，大致由西向東平穩劃過天空，約幾分鐘就走完。It doesn’t blink, glides roughly west to east, and crosses the sky in a few minutes.
    5. 看着它「消失」Watch it vanish有時它會在半空慢慢變暗消失，那是它飛進了地球的影子。Sometimes it fades out mid-sky. That’s the moment it flies into Earth’s shadow.
